# Milestone 13 — Roku Plex Validation

## Goal

Validate SwiftyTranscoder output through the Plex application on the Roku TV, the final playback environment named in the original project brief.

## Step 1: Representative television episode

Use `Reacher - s04e08 - Cut.mp4` first. It represents HEVC Main with the `hvc1` tag, AC-3 5.1 at 48 kHz and 224 kb/s, protected +6 dB gain, and burned forced-English subtitles.

Record Plex's separate video and audio decisions, picture quality, dialogue level, subtitle appearance and timing, synchronization, seeking, and chapter behavior.

The user completed the representative Reacher test in the Plex application on the Roku TV and confirmed that playback was very good and all requested checks passed. Together with the earlier Safari/MacBook and Firefox/Rossum/HomePod results, this completes the original Plex client matrix. Milestone 13 is complete.
